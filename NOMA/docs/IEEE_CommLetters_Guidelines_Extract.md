## Binding Constraints

### Page & Length
- **Page limit:** 5 pages (standard two-column IEEE format) — **Binding**
- **Overlength:** Mandatory Overlength Page Charges (MOPC) for pages beyond the limit — **Binding**
- **Word count:** No explicit word count limit stated; the 5-page limit in IEEE format is the binding constraint — **[VERIFY]**

### Abstract
- **Format:** Unstructured (no section headings) — **Binding**
- **Word limit:** ~150 words — **[VERIFY exact number from live portal]**
- **Content:** Must include problem, method, result, implication as a self-contained paragraph — **Advisory**

### Template & Formatting
- **Template:** IEEE two-column journal format — **Binding**
- **Word template:** Available at https://template-selector.ieee.org/ (select IEEE Transactions template) — **[DOWNLOAD and save to Manuscript/templates/]**
- **Font:** Times New Roman 10pt (standard IEEE) — **[VERIFY]**
- **Columns:** Two-column — **Binding**

### Keywords
- **Count:** 3–5 index terms — **Binding**
- **Source:** IEEE Thesaurus (https://www.ieee.org/publications/services/thesaurus.html) — **Binding**

### Figures & Artwork
- **Minimum DPI:** 300 DPI for halftone/photos; 600 DPI for line art — **Binding (upon acceptance)**
- **Formats accepted:** EPS, TIFF, PDF, PNG (high-res) — **Binding**
- **Review submission:** Figures embedded in the PDF for review — **Binding**
- **Color:** RGB for online publication; must be legible in B&W print — **Binding**
- **Font in figures:** ≥8pt — **[VERIFY]**

### Tables
- **Caption placement:** Above the table — **Binding**
- **Caption style:** `TABLE I` (Roman numeral, small caps) — **Binding**
- **Vertical lines:** Discouraged — **Advisory**

### References & Citations
- **Style:** IEEE numbered [1], [2], … — **Binding**
- **DOI:** Required where available — **Binding**
- **Format:** `[N] A. Initials Surname, "Title," *Journal Abbrev.*, vol. X, no. Y, pp. Z–ZZ, Mon. Year, doi: 10.xxxx/xxxxx.` — **Binding**
- **Reference limit:** No hard cap stated; 15–25 typical for Letters — **Advisory [VERIFY]**
